Job Description

The Applied Machine Learning Scientist will perform a variety of activities, which may include:

    Apply advanced machine learning methods to conduct data-driven research, identify opportunities, and make tangible recommendations to senior management. 
    Collaborate with business partners to identify opportunities for process optimization and informed decision-making and build innovative solutions. 
    Work with structured and unstructured data and prepare data sets for machine learning through integration, cleaning, feature engineering, scaling and transforming data 
    Build production-scale solutions and work with deployment teams to integrate in business operations 
    Leverage visualization methods to represent results and communicate outcomes to technical and non-technical audiences 

Job Requirements

    Must be graduated between May 2025 and December 2026 with a Graduate degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering, and/or Data Analytics or related. 
    Strong statistical and analytical skills as well as a passion for analytics are necessary. 
    Experience with analyzing large data sets, drawing insights and inferences, and communicating outputs. 
    Experienced in one or more of: 
    Big Data – Programming, Data/Text Mining, Optimization, Algorithm Development, Automation, Data Visualization, Simulation 
    Machine Learning – Predictive Analytics, Regression, Logit Modeling, Model development, Discrete Choice, Propensity Modeling, Natural Language Processing, Generative AI. 
    Software Engineering – Code Quality, Version Control, Object-Oriented Programming 
    Strong technical knowledge and experience with one or more of: Python, R, Spark, SQL, SAS, Java, or C/C++ 
    Technical knowledge or experience with Cloud environments as well as experience with Data Management, and Project Management are a plus. 
    Must have strong leadership qualities including demonstrating initiative, establishing priorities and using strong communication, analytical and problem-solving skills to add value to each initiative, project or team assigned to. 
    Able to participate as part of a team with targeted deliverables, working on initiatives designed to improve decisions, processes, systems, and applications which support our key businesses. 
    Remain current with emerging trends and technologies in Data Science
