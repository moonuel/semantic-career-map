About The Opportunity

We are seeking a technical AI Product Intern to support our GenAI and AI Agent product initiatives. In this role, you will work directly with the AI product team to develop prototypes that spark future product innovation, as well as help improve model performance, evaluate multilingual model output quality. Furthermore, you will have the opportunity to help us evolve our product analytics capabilities in Pendo, and craft meaningful content in Confluence. This internship is ideal for a Computer Science or Engineering student with a passion for machine learning, AI, experimentation, and turning messy problems into working solutions.
